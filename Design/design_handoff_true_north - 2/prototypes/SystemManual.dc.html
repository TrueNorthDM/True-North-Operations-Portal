Overview

What this portal is

The Operations Portal is True North’s single home for the tools, dashboards, and to-do lists that keep publishing and client work moving. It doesn’t replace Monday, WordPress or QuickBooks Time — it reads from them and gives the team one calm, consistent place to see status and act on it.

Who signs in
True North staff and contractors on the allow-list — no public access.
How you get in
Magic-link email — no password to remember or reset.
Where data lives
Monday, WordPress and QuickBooks Time. The portal reads — it’s the source that’s the truth.

Permissions

Access model

Access is never granted to one person at a time. People join teams; teams are granted a role on projects. Everything is managed from the Directory.

People
Staff & contractors with a portal login
join
Teams
SEO, Writing, Web, Leadership
granted role on
Projects
AAAF, HFA and other client sites
Manager
Full access to the project: edit pages, assign work, and manage the team’s access.
Editor
Works inside the project — edits pages and moves items through the workflow.
Viewer
Read-only — can see dashboards and status but can’t change anything.
Login is separate from access. Being on the sign-in allow-list only lets someone in the front door — what they see inside is decided entirely by their team’s project grants.
Data flow

How Monday feeds the portal

Monday.com is the system of record for work. The portal connects with an API token (set in Settings → API tokens), pulls the boards it needs on a schedule, and shows them as clean, read-only views. A few actions write back to Monday.

Data sources

Three systems feed the portal. Credentials for each live in Settings.

Monday.com
System of record for publishing, projects and assignments. Connected by API token.
WordPress
The AAAF and HFA sites where hreflang tags are published. Edited by hand from Projects.
QuickBooks Time
Hours and cost behind the i7 Budget Usage Report. Every figure traces back to a timesheet.
