How the i7 Budget Usage Report is built

The report never invents a number. It combines one thing pulled from QuickBooks Time with three things you set in Settings — then only adds and subtracts.

The four inputs
Actuals

QuickBooks Time (TSheets)

The real hours. Pulled automatically every 4 hours. Each approved entry says who worked, on which i7 code (→ project + job code), on which activity, and how many hours.

Rates

Hourly rates

Settings → People & Rates. Each person’s $/hour on i7, with history ($120 through January, $95 from February, foreign contractors $60).

Budget $

Monthly budget

Settings → Clients & Budgets → i7. Each month’s budget in dollars (base + extra).

Budget hrs

Budget hours per project

Same i7 modal. The budgeted hours for each project, per month.

How each column is calculated (for the selected month)
Hours used
= sum of that group’s hours
TSHEETS
Budget used ($)
= for each person: their hours × their rate that month, summed
TSHEETS × RATES
Budget hrs
= the project’s budgeted hours for the month
SETTINGS
Hrs avail.
= Budget hrs – Hours used
DERIVED
Budget avail. ($)
= Hrs avail. × the month’s standard i7 rate ($95 in July)
DERIVED × RATES
Person columns
= each person’s hours in that group
TSHEETS
Budget balance (the running balance at the top)

It works like a bank account, running from January:

Carried in = (all monthly budgets since January) – (all actual cost since January), for the months before the one you’re viewing.

Budget total this month = Carried in + this month’s budget (base + extra).

Used = the same “Budget used” total from the bottom of the table.

Balance left = Budget total – Used (this carries into next month).
