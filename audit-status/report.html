What changed, in five lines

Who sees what

Every page and every action decides on the server who may see or change it. The database refuses everything else: salaries, rates, personal contacts, Monday, Href Links, publishing and settings tables are closed to signed-in sessions and read only by the portal, after it has decided. A daily health check, "Database protections", fails the moment one of them opens again.

Numbers you can trust

Reads that silently stopped at 1,000 rows are gone (the Report saw 2,442 hours out of 7,757). A failed read now says so instead of showing $0, zeros or an empty list. Invoice lines add up to the cent, shares add up to 100%, hours are counted per person, and weeks and days follow New York.

Monday and the integrations

A bad Monday answer no longer empties a board, the change log and the board are written together, the webhook acts on what Monday says now and processes each delivery once, and 695 false events were moved to reserve tables. QuickBooks Time calls stop after 30 seconds instead of hanging.

Watching itself

Health runs every 4 hours, including Monday events, the daily summary, publishing snapshot and the database protections, and a check that could not run counts as a failure. The Home banner and Automations read one health summary.

One way of doing things

Portal dialogs instead of the browser's, dropdowns instead of native selects, actions that answer with a sentence instead of crashing, one CSV and month-name module, and lint clean.
