# Messages to the team about the move

Drafts, approved by Arnaldo before they go out (Slack). Words in [brackets]
are filled in on the day.

## A. Before switch-over day (`p6-tell`, about 3 days before)

```
Heads-up: the Operations Portal is moving to its new home 🏡

On [Saturday, October 10], the Operations Portal moves to True North's own server and gets a new address: truenorthportal.online

What to expect
• From [Saturday morning], the portal will be paused while everything is copied over. You'll see a "We're moving" page. It should be back within a couple of days.
• Nothing is lost: your work, reports and settings all come along.
• If you need anything from the portal (a report, an export), please get it before [Friday evening].

After the move
• Go to truenorthportal.online and sign in with your GoodNews email, as usual. Everyone signs in once more, since sessions don't carry over.
• Old links and bookmarks take you to the new address automatically, but it's worth updating your bookmark.

Questions? Just reply here. Thanks!
```

## B. When it is back (`p6-announce`)

```
The Operations Portal is back, at its new home: truenorthportal.online ✅

As you know, we've been moving the portal onto True North's own setup: its own domain, its own server, and its own database, instead of services under other accounts. Most of that work is now done, and you can sign back in.

How to sign in
• Go to truenorthportal.online and sign in with your GoodNews email. You'll get an email with a 6-digit code: type it in, or press the button in the email.
• If you had set up two-step sign-in, your authenticator app works as before.
• Everyone signs in once more, since the old sessions didn't come along.

What stays the same
• Your work, reports, settings and history are all there, exactly as you left them.
• Old links and bookmarks take you to the new address automatically, but it's worth updating your bookmark.

We'll keep an eye on everything over the next few days and finish a few last details behind the scenes. If something looks off or doesn't work, tell me right away.

Thanks for your patience during the move!
```

## C. Two-step sign-in for everyone who already had access (`p7-2fa-announce`, phase 8)

Cutoff set on 5 October 2026: the week runs from Tuesday, October 6 to Tuesday,
October 13 (midnight US Eastern). Attach the screenshots of the setup screen
with the QR code and of the yellow reminder.

```
New: two-step sign-in for the Operations Portal 🔐

To keep the portal (and our clients' data) safe, signing in will also ask for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone.

Set it up once, it takes a minute:
1. Install an authenticator app if you don't have one. Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, iPhone Passwords or 1Password all work.
2. Sign in to the portal. It shows the setup screen right away; you'll also find it in the yellow reminder at the top ("Set it up now") and in My profile.
3. Scan the QR code with the app and type the 6-digit code it shows.

From then on, after the email code, the portal asks for the code from your app.

You have one week: until Tuesday, October 13. Until then you can press "Skip for now". After that, the portal asks you to set it up before you can go on.

New or lost phone? Ask Arnaldo or Tina and we'll reset it for you.
```
