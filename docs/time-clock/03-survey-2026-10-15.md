## 2. Clocky as built

### Data model (migration 057 onwards)
- **tc_settings**: one row. Timezone, week start, period type, rounding,
  auto clock-out hours, midnight split, overtime, daily/weekly regular hours,
  import_cutoff_date.
- **tc_jobcodes**: id, external_id (TSheets id), parent_id (the tree), name,
  kind (regular, pto, unpaid), billable, active. Written ONLY by the import.
- **tc_fields / tc_field_options**: the TSheets custom fields (1 Company,
  2 Project, 3 Activity, 4 Billable, ...) and their values. Written ONLY by the
  import. tc_entries.fields is keyed by tc_fields.id.
- **tc_entries**: person, jobcode, started_at/ended_at (nullable),
  duration_seconds, entry_date, kind (clock, manual, absence), state (open,
  submitted, approved, locked), source (portal, tsheets), notes, fields,
  external_id, created_by, manual_batch/manual_line (101). One running clock per
  person (unique index).
- **tc_entry_audit**: written on own changes, never read by any screen.
- **tc_periods**: monthly; closed_at unused.
- **tc_submissions**: one per person per month (draft, submitted, approved,
  returned).
- **tc_absence_requests**; **tc_absence_balances** (never written or read).
- **tc_import_runs**, **tc_reconciliation**: import bookkeeping.
- Report side: tc_project_purchases(_log), tc_project_donations(_log),
  tc_user_links, monday_boards.tc_jobcode_id.
- No client or project entity: a client is a top-level job code; the project is
  the value of custom field "2 Project". Billing clients live in tr_clients,
  synced from TSheets job codes. Rates: tr_assignments by person.

### What works
- **Employee (My Hours, /hours)**: clock in/out with job code, required fields
  and notes; manual entries (one day, max 24h); edit/delete own OPEN portal rows;
  week view with day/week totals and a month total; submit a MONTH; request and
  cancel time off.
- **Approver**: approve or hand back a month (Super Admins only, totals only);
  approve or deny time off; reopen (only offered on your own month).
- **Admin**: rules form (saved, mostly not applied); manual import
  (full/delta/recheck); reconciliation table.
- Reports and approvals pages of Clocky are "coming soon".

### Missing or half built
- Not applied: rounding, auto clock-out, midnight split, overtime, period type,
  overlap check, balances.
- No job code / field / client management in the portal.
- No breaks, no payroll export, no reminders, no approval queue across months,
  approver cannot see entries, no admin edit of others, no week submit, no
  balances shown, no clock-first mobile view.
- Copy and access are pre-cutover: "QuickBooks Time is still the official
  record"; my-hours page opens to Admins only by default (catalogue v [1,0,0]).

New, found Oct 15:
- **N1** stopClock has no maximum: a forgotten clock records 60+ hours.
- **N2** The running clock is only looked for in the displayed week.
- **N3** Absence rows from approved time off are open/portal, so editable; an
  edit turns them into 'manual' and cancel no longer removes them.
- **N4** The picker offers PTO/unpaid codes and folders as work.
- **N5** Admins bypass "Super Admin approves" through RLS (is_admin FOR ALL).
- **N6** Reopen sets imported rows to open: they leave the Report for good after
  the cut-over.
- **N7** Errors swallowed when entries follow a decision (submit, approve,
  return, reopen, absence rows).
- **N8** Approved time off writes entries into submitted/approved months.
- **N9** No approval queue across months.
- **N10** Imported entries on inactive codes show "-".
- **N11** Nothing stops the TSheets crons at cut-over; a past cutoff cannot be
  saved.
- **N12** Month totals mix TSheets and portal rows (double during a parallel
  period).
- **N13** A month can be "submitted" with only imported rows.
- **N14** Return writes approved_by.
- **N15** Some audit lines describe changes that did not happen.
- **N16** Job code not filtered by the Company/Project dropdowns.

## 4. Everything that depends on TSheets

### Touch points
- `lib/tsheets/client.ts`: users, job codes (fetchTimesheets is dead code).
- `lib/tsheets/sync.ts` (cron tsheets-sync, "Sync now"): tr_clients from job
  codes; people matched by name, tsheets_user_id, tc_user_links, new
  tsheets_only people.
- `lib/timeclock/source.ts` + `import.ts` + `runner.ts` (cron clocky-delta,
  manual import): tc_jobcodes, tc_fields, tc_field_options, people,
  tc_user_links, tc_entries, tc_reconciliation, deletions.
- `lib/health/checks.ts` checkTsheets (down without a token: red banner and
  "integration failed" notification every 4h).
- `/api/admin/tsheets-inventory` (diagnostic; only caller of grant-pass.ts).
- Token: app_settings.tsheets_api_token; getTsheetsStatus (configured,
  tsheets_synced_at).

### What breaks or freezes when the sync stops
1. Job codes, field options and clients freeze (no portal writer).
2. People are no longer created from TSheets (fine; the Linked column and the
   "link in Settings" path become meaningless).
3. Removing the token: i7 shows MOCK data, health goes down, crons fail or skip.
4. Keeping the token with a closed account: every delta fails, reports say
   "import failed".
5. Freshness badges freeze.
6. Reopen strands imported rows; imported rows left open/submitted at the last
   delta can never be approved in the portal.
7. i7 and Project Hours count every state; the Report counts approved only.
8. The Rules form cannot be saved once the cutoff is in the past.
9. PTO balances never migrated (tc_absence_balances empty; /timeoff_requests is
   404 in the TSheets API).
10. **Rippling payroll pulls hours from TSheets with its own token, outside the
    portal. Owner unknown. It gets nothing after the switch.**

### To read Clocky only
1. Catalogue management in the portal: job codes (parent, kind, billable,
   active), fields and options, clients (tr_clients portal-owned, i7 lookup off
   tsheets_jobcode_id).
2. Remove the i7 "configured" gate (no mock data without a token).
3. Freshness from Clocky's own activity (newest tc_entries.updated_at; all
   portal rows on Project Hours); drop or relabel the TSheets sync badge.
4. Retire the TSheets crons, health check, automations entries and copy.
5. Cut-over mechanics: day-exact cutoff (or cut over on the 1st), allow an
   unchanged past cutoff, final full pull, then remove the token.
6. Approvals for imported rows: approve/lock all TSheets entries before the last
   delta; Reopen leaves source='tsheets' rows alone; decide who approves;
   decide whether client reports count approved hours only.
7. PTO balances: seed from TSheets pto_balances or drop the plan.
8. Payroll (phase 6): a Rippling feed from tc_entries; find the owner.
9. Copy: every "hours come from QuickBooks Time" text, the token card, the
   Directory TSheets column/pill/unlink checkbox. Keep tsheets_user_id,
   tc_user_links and external_id as history.

## 5. Proposed order (Oct 15)

1. Make the clock safe: C1-C3, C5-C7, N1-N8, N11-N13, auto clock-out and a
   maximum, server and database locks on submitted/approved months.
2. The catalogue becomes the portal's (job codes, fields, clients).
3. Day-one features: approvals queue with entries, admin edits with history,
   reminders, payroll export, My Hours open to everyone.
4. Reports read Clocky only (gate, freshness, state rule, copy).
5. Pilot on staging with 2 or 3 people.
6. Cut-over day: TSheets fully approved, final full import, crons and checks off,
   Clocky open to all.

Decisions asked on Oct 15 (open): cut-over date (suggested Monday Nov 2, 2026),
Rippling owner and payroll format, who approves (Super Admins or Team Leaders),
month or week submit, client reports count all or approved hours, breaks,
PTO balances.
