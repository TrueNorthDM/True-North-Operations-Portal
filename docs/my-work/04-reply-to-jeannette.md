**Subject:** My Work: what changed from your feedback

Hi Jeannette,

Thanks for the detailed feedback on My Work. Here's what you asked, what changed, and how to use it. If My Work is already open, refresh the page.

**1. Clicking a task should open Monday, with the history as a secondary option.**
Done. Each row now starts with an eye icon that opens the item in Monday, and a clock icon next to it that opens the history.

**2. 700+ tasks, most not active or relevant, and none of them your priorities.**
A step now counts only when its status column has its own deadline, and each step uses its own status and deadline. Your open steps went from 451 to 348, and late ones from 273 to 172. For priorities, see Team priority below.

**3. Non-English AAAF pages should skip On-page SEO.**
You're right, something is off with that automation. I'm still investigating it.

**4. Old boards and tasks with statuses not recognized as done.**
Status columns without their own deadline, and ones like Kick-Off, no longer create steps. "Not Relevant" now counts as done on BM - Photo Curation Audits. To find stale items, use the "Any last change" filter and pick "No change in 6+ months" or "12+ months". Other boards that need the same setting, and old boards to archive, are on our Monday cleanup list.

**5. All of someone's tasks in one place, to help balance workloads.**
Click Everyone, then pick a person or a team. A side-by-side workload view for team leaders is planned.

**Also new: filters and sorting.**
Use the filters at the top (status, board, due date and last change), and click a column heading to sort. The Columns button hides the columns you don't need.

## Team priority (new)

SEO can now have one priority order, and as its team leader you set it.

1. On My Work, click **Team priority**, next to Mine and Everyone.
2. Move a step with the arrows, by dragging it, or by clicking its number and typing a new place.
3. It saves as you go.

Only the steps you move keep their place. The rest follow board priority and deadline, so they still move when a deadline changes in Monday. "Reset to the default order" undoes everything. The Team column shows each step's place in the SEO list.

Who follows it: everyone on SEO. If someone's main team has an order of its own, their list follows that one first, and anyone on more than one team can switch between their teams from the line above their list.

If anything looks wrong, send me the item name and I'll check it.
