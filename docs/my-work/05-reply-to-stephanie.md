## Team priority (new)

Graphics can now have one priority order, and as its team leader you set it.

1. On My Work, click **Team priority**, next to Mine and Everyone.
2. Move a step with the arrows, by dragging it, or by clicking its number and typing a new place.
3. It saves as you go.

Only the steps you move keep their place. The rest follow board priority and deadline, so they still move when a deadline changes in Monday. "Reset to the default order" undoes everything. The Team column shows each step's place in the Graphics list.

Who follows it: everyone on Graphics. If someone's main team has an order of its own, their list follows that one first (Cheyenne's main team is Writers), and anyone on more than one team can switch between their teams from the line above their list.
