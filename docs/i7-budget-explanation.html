The spreadsheet today

Hours are the anchor

You allocate the budgeted hours per project. Those hours, times the rate, produce the dollar budget. That’s why February–June land at $44,975 — it’s the result of the hours you set.

budgeted hours → × rate → $ budget
The app going forward

Dollars are the anchor

The dollar budget is the ceiling you set. Hours become the plan. And actual usage is read live from QuickBooks Time — each person’s real hours × their own rate, on its own.

$ budget (ceiling) + real hours × rate → live usage

Same inputs, same output. The app just reads the clock for you instead of asking you to re-key it every month.
