# Annual review workflow (owner, 10 November 2026)

The owner's draft, the mockup "APR Workflow" (version 2) and his answers to it. This replaces the
handoff of 3 November where they differ. Everything here is decided; open points are marked.

## The calendar

- The VP of Operations starts the reviews (Super Admins as back-up). A window explains the steps and
  confirms the year; only the current year is offered.
- From the second Monday of November, the VP has "Start the <year> reviews" on Needs you until it is done.
- Every due date comes from the kickoff day (K). With K = Mon, Nov 9, 2026 everything is approved by Tue, Dec 15:

| Due | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| K+2 (Wed) | Company Priorities for next year | CISO |
| K+4 (Fri) | VP of Operations' priorities for next year | VP |
| K+11 (Fri) | Self-review, everyone (Team Leaders write their team's priorities first) | Everyone |
| K+16 (Wed) | Review written and meeting booked, both lanes | VP for Team Leaders, Team Leaders for members |
| K+25 (Fri) | Leaders lane: meeting held, read and agreed | VP and Team Leaders |
| K+30 (Wed) | Teams lane: meeting held, read and agreed | Team Leaders and members |
| K+32 (Fri) | Leaders lane: final remarks and approval (Approve all) | VP |
| K+36 (Tue) | Teams lane: final remarks and approval (Approve all) | Team Leaders |

- Everyone's self-review opens at the kickoff. The look back at the year needs nobody; the goals for
  next year open when the CISO's and the VP's priorities are marked done (or their due date passed).
- A Team Leader writes their team's priorities at the start of their own self-review. No approval step.
  The self-review cannot be sent without at least one team priority.
- Dates rule: a later date can be moved earlier when the step before it finished early (the progress
  block offers "Move the later dates up by N days"); when a step runs late, the next dates stay.
- The 8 typed dates of Company settings > Human Resources go away.

## Lanes and two reviewers

- Leaders lane: people with no Team Leader (the Team Leaders and anyone reviewed by who they report to).
- Teams lane: people reviewed by their Team Leader.
- Two reviewers: someone who leads their main team and is a plain member of another team led by someone
  else (Kathleen: leads Social Media, member of SEO under Jeannette; Stephanie; Joy). Their first
  reviewer is who they report to (leaders lane); the second is the other team's leader (teams lane).
  Not for the VP and the CISO. Company settings can add or remove a second reviewer by hand.
- One self-review; each reviewer has their own part: their answers, meeting, read and agree, approval.
  Each goal says which reviewer it is for; each reviewer edits only their own goals.
- One joint meeting is offered: booking with both reviewers links the event to both parts.
- The review is approved when every part is; only then the PDF goes to Drive and the person is told.

## After approval

- Needs you: "Your <year> review is approved" with "See my review". The window says the goals are on
  the profile and in Human Resources > My review, that a copy is in Drive, and that a check-in comes in
  6 months. "Got it" clears the row.
- 6 months after the kickoff, the VP gets "Start the 6-month check-in".

## 6-month check-in

1. The VP and the CISO go through the priorities: no change, changed (with what and why) or added.
2. "Priorities checked, tell everyone": each person and each reviewer get the check-in on Needs you.
3. Each reviewer books the meeting (VP with Team Leaders, Team Leaders with members).
4. On the review, the check-in tab: tick goals done, adjust or add goals, notes from each side; the
   reviewer finishes it. Due dates from the start S: priorities S+4, meetings booked S+11, done S+25.

## Who is in

Employees, plus anyone added in "Who takes part" (Stephanie Aguirre, a contractor, takes part).
