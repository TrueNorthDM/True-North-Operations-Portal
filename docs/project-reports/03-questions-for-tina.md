# Questions for Tina

Written to be sent as they are. Each one says why it is being asked and what
happens either way, so none of them needs a follow-up to be answerable.

Questions 1 and 2 are the ones that block work. The rest can wait.

---

## 1. Should donations be recorded one by one, or as a monthly total?

**Why I am asking.** Right now the portal stores one figure per project per
month: you type what came in for AAAF in September and that is what the report
shows. It works, and it is the least typing.

What it cannot do is answer **when** something was donated, or show a donation
on its own. The month is the finest date it holds, and correcting a figure
replaces the old one with no record of the change.

Arnaldo has since asked for a report of how much was donated, when, and to which
project, and that needs the donations kept separately rather than added together
before they are stored.

**Two ways to go:**

**A. Keep monthly totals.** You total the cheques and online giving yourself,
as you do now, and type one figure per project. Fastest to enter. No dates, no
individual donations, and no report beyond the monthly one.

**B. One line per donation.** You enter each one with its date, the project it
is for, and the amount. The monthly figure on the report becomes those added up,
so nothing you see today changes. It makes the donations report possible, and it
means a mistake can be corrected without losing what was there before.

**What I need to know:** is entering a line per donation realistic for how the
figures reach you? If they arrive as a bank statement or a list you would rather
not retype, say so and we will stay with totals.

*Whichever you choose, the portal keeps working as it does today. This only
decides whether the donations report is possible.*

## 2. If donations are recorded one by one, do you want the donor's name on them?

**Why I am asking.** This is a privacy question, not a technical one.

At the moment no donor is named anywhere, which is why anyone who can open the
report can read the donation figures: they are sums of money, not personal
information.

The moment a donor's name is stored, that stops being true. It becomes personal
data, and we would need to decide who is allowed to see it, which is a different
and tighter rule than the one the report uses now.

**What I need to know:** do you need the donor on the record, or is the date,
the amount and the project enough for what you and Ed need?

If you do need it, that is fine. It just needs deciding up front rather than
added later.

## 3. Do Grants Pass Church or School ever receive donations?

**Why I am asking.** Your layout gives all four projects a donations column, but
Church and School are the two that buy hours in advance at $90 rather than being
donation funded.

If they never receive donations, those two cells will be blank forever, and the
report can say so rather than leaving them looking unanswered.

If they sometimes do, they stay exactly as they are.

## 4. Should the unrestricted fund be part of the Totals line?

**Why I am asking.** Unrestricted money now has its own row under the four
projects, since it belongs to none of them.

Today the Totals line adds the four projects **and** the fund together, so it is
everything that came in. The Unrestricted row sits directly above it, so the
arithmetic is visible.

The alternative is to total only the four projects and leave the fund outside,
so the total is strictly "money for projects".

**What I need to know:** which of those two is the number you would want to copy
into a financial report for Ed?

## Not a question, just so you know

Two things you asked for are already in and worth trying:

- **Money in** on the report toolbar takes both donations and the Grants Pass
  prepaid hours. Leaving a field empty means "nothing entered" and shows blank on
  the report; typing `0` means "nothing came in" and shows `$0.00`. They are
  deliberately different, since the report is copied into a financial report.
- **Export** now offers the client and internal versions as PDF and CSV. The
  CSVs cover whatever period is on screen; the PDFs are one month, so they stay
  greyed out until a single month is chosen.
