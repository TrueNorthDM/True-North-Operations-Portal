# Questions for Tina

Five things. The first two are holding up work; the rest can wait.

**1. Donations: one line each, or one total per month?**
Today the portal takes one figure per project per month. That cannot say *when*
something was donated, and we have been asked for a report of how much came in,
when, and for which project. That needs each donation on its own line with a
date. Is entering them one by one workable, or do the figures reach you as a
total you would rather not break up?

**2. If one by one, does the donor's name go on it?**
No donor is named anywhere today, which is why everyone who can open the report
can see the figures. A name makes it personal data and we would have to limit who
can read it. Do you need it?

**3. Do Grants Pass Church or School ever receive donations?**
They buy hours in advance rather than being donation funded. If they never
receive any, those cells stay blank forever and the report can say so.

**4. Should the unrestricted fund be inside the Totals line?**
It has its own row now. The total currently adds the four projects and the fund,
so it is everything that came in. Keep it that way, or total the projects only?

**5. Do you want a note against a figure?**
Something like "October appeal". The portal can store one, the form does not
offer it yet. Useful or clutter?
