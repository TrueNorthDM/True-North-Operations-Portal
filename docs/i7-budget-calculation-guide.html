i7 Budget Usage Report

How the i7 Budget Usage Report is built

The report never invents a number. It combines one thing pulled from QuickBooks Time with three things you set in Settings, then only adds and subtracts.

The four inputs

Actuals

QuickBooks Time (TSheets)

The real hours. Pulled automatically every 4 hours. Each approved entry says who worked, on which i7 code (→ project + job code), on which activity, and how many hours.

Rates

Hourly rates

Settings → People & Rates. Each person’s $/hour on i7, with history ($120 through January, $95 from February, foreign contractors $60).

Budget $

Monthly budget

Settings → Clients & Budgets → i7. Each month’s budget in dollars (base + extra). This is the source of truth for the month, the report never back-calculates it from hours.

Budget hrs

Budget hours per project

Same i7 modal. A planning estimate: how the budget splits into hours per project. Editing it never changes the dollar budget, a live “left to allocate” line flags when the hours run past it. Copy a month across to the others in a click.

How each column is calculated (for the selected month)

Hours used= sum of that group’s hoursTSheets
Budget used ($)= for each person: their hours × their rate that month, summedTSheets × Rates
Budget hrs= the project’s budgeted hours for the monthSettings
Hrs avail.= Budget hrs − Hours usedDerived
Budget avail. ($)= Hrs avail. × the month’s standard i7 rate ($95 in July)Derived × Rates
Person columns= each person’s hours in that groupTSheets

Budget balance (the running balance at the top)

It works like a bank account, running from January:

  • Carried in = (all monthly budgets since January) − (all actual cost since January), for the months before the one you’re viewing.
  • Budget total this month = Carried in + this month’s budget (base + extra).
  • Used = the same “Budget used” total from the bottom of the table.
  • Balance left = Budget total − Used (this carries into next month).
On the report, Budget total this month shows the breakdown in brackets, e.g. $49,094 ($55,000 − $5,906): this month’s full budget minus what was carried over (or plus, if last month came in under). The Team Update button (next to Export) turns the same numbers (used, % used, and what’s left) into a shareable image for the team.

In one line

Settings sets the targets (budgeted hours and dollars), QuickBooks Time brings the actuals (hours), the rates turn hours into dollars, and the report just adds and subtracts, every number traceable.
